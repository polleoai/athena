## Pinned-issue / forum / Discord-channel update

**Athena v1.8.24 released — whole papers, honest captures, safer by default**

The first public release since 1.8.18; it also brings the changes from 1.8.19 to 1.8.23.

What you'll notice as a user:

1. **Papers keep their figures**, saved next to the note and shown at their captions. Older papers can get theirs with `kb backfill-figures`.
2. **Whole papers.** Long PDFs used to stop at 50,000 characters. New captures are complete, and `kb backfill-fulltext` restores the missing text of papers you saved before. Both commands only add, never remove, and `kb undo` reverts them.
3. **No silent half-captures.** When Athena has to fall back to a weaker source, it prints one warning with the reason and marks the note, so `kb lint` can list it for a re-capture.
4. **One source, one note.** Saving a paper again (its abstract page, then its PDF) updates the existing note instead of making a twin. `kb merge-raw` folds an existing twin into its page.
5. **Safer by default.** Pages and redirects that point at `localhost`, your router or cloud-metadata addresses are refused on every capture path.
6. **The built-in assistant is Gryphon 2.13.1**, with the newest Claude models in its list and a fix for spending caps.

Upgrade: update the plugin in Obsidian, then run `kb doctor` once.

## Substack / newsletter paragraph

This release is about trusting what you save. A paper now arrives whole: every page of its text, and its figures in place. Papers you saved before can catch up with two commands that only add, never remove. When a capture can't do its best (a missing browser, a site that blocks automated reading), Athena no longer saves a thinner copy and stays quiet: it tells you, marks the note, and lists it for a re-capture later. And captured pages can no longer steer Athena toward your own computer or home network.
